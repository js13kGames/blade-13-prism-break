## Sever the unicorn network

**BLADE:13 - PRISM BREAK** is a one-thumb neon survivors-like built entirely in 13KB.

UNICORN, the city's rainbow-energy AI, has corrupted every machine into prism beasts. Play a standing chibi cyber-unicorn, cut through the seven-color swarm, and trigger PRISM BREAK before the BLACK UNICORN ends the run.

### Controls
- **Desktop:** WASD/arrow keys to move, Space to Blink, F or Shift to trigger PRISM BREAK, 1-3 to pick upgrades, M to mute.
- **Mobile:** drag anywhere to move, tap to Blink, and press the on-screen PRISM BREAK button when charged.

### Highlights
- Seven-color blades, rainbow Blink ghosts, prism rings, and a BLACK UNICORN boss.
- Procedural infinite city, seven enemy families, upgrade cards, and escalating threat waves.
- Canvas 2D only: no images, audio files, libraries, or external requests at runtime.
- Procedural synth soundtrack and sound effects.
- Responsive desktop/mobile layout with a 320x200 pixel pipeline.
